Tap when a contraction begins, tap again when it ends. That's the whole job. Mis-taps get fixed in History — swipe to delete, Undo takes back the last touch.

Interval is start-to-start

"Every 5 minutes" measures the gap between the beginnings of contractions — the way providers measure. Duration is how long each lasts; Pattern is how long this stretch has been going.

The timeline reads downward

Newest at the top, the gap between each pair printed right where it happened. Numbers show nothing until there's enough to mean something — a dash is honest, a guess isn't.
