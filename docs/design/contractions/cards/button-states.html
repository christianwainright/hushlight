RULES

  • A circle, the category's shape — someone arriving from any other contraction timer already knows what it is. Ours grows with Dynamic Type instead of clipping: content sizes the circle, 216 pt is the floor.
  • The label is always the action, never the state. "Start" / "Tap when it ends" — never "STOPPED".
  • Figure and ground swap on state. Idle: the button is loud and the word is the content. Running: the fill goes quiet, a tint ring holds the edge, and the count becomes the content.
  • It never moves. Bottom-anchored above the safe area on an opaque bar; the readout scrolls behind it. Mid-contraction aim is zero.
  • Heavy haptic on every hit — confirmation without looking.
