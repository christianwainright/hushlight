# The Hushlight identity — research record (2026-08-23)

The naming and visual-identity research that produced **Hushlight** and the
**Nightstand** direction, run the day of the extraction (decision 0033).

- `name-research.json` — round 1: 29 candidates from four angles, top 6 vetted
  against the App Store, USPTO, domains, and dark associations. Every warm
  dictionary word was already mined out; the vetters' unanimous fix was a coinage.
  (Round 2's winning verdict — Hushlight clear on every axis, runner-up
  Soonrise — is summarized in decision 0033 and BACKLOG HD-1.)
- `design-directions.json` — competitor teardowns and the three drafted identities
  (Nightstand 44/50, First Light 43, Slack Tide 33) with the judge's scores and
  the ten grafts folded into the winner. The "Nightstand" entry is the buildable
  spec: full light/dark palette with contrast floors, type strategy, ember-tape
  motif, motion, icon concept, voice strings.
- `*.dc.html` + `canvas.json` — the design-canvas artboards: brand board, first
  launch, timer (resting / mid-contraction), verdict, history, daytime
  translation, and the First Light alternate. The live, editable canvas is the
  "Hushlight Identity" artifact linked from decision 0033.
