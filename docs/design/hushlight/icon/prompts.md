# Image generation prompts

Built-in `image_gen` tool, 2026-09-05. Final original outputs are saved as the source masters in this directory. The light and tinted edits both reference `source-dark.png`. The initial dome concept was rejected by the user for reading as a mushroom; these prompts describe the corrected classic lamp.

## Dark / marketing

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: finished iOS app icon artwork for Hushlight, a calm contraction timer for nighttime use.
Primary request: Create a beautiful, immediately recognizable classic TABLE LAMP glowing warmly against a deep warm near-black background. The silhouette MUST read as a bedside table lamp at a glance. Use a traditional tapered lampshade, a clearly separate straight narrow upright stem, and a low flat round base. The previous design was rejected for looking like a mushroom, so DO NOT use a dome, hemisphere, mushroom cap, organic flared stalk, or one-piece toadstool shape.
Geometry: FRONT VIEW of one compact lamp, centered. A classic softly rounded TRAPEZOID lampshade, with a broad almost-flat horizontal top, gently sloping STRAIGHT sides, and a wider horizontal hem. The shade is a truncated cone, NOT a dome and NOT a bell. Shade top width around 34% of the canvas, bottom width around 64%, shade height around 34%. The top of the shade is about 17% down from the top of the image, its hem about 51% down. A gently curved elliptical lower opening makes the shade visibly hollow; a small visible upper elliptical rim can suggest depth. Below the shade there is open black negative space around a CLEARLY SEPARATE straight cylindrical bronze stem, about 8% of canvas wide and 20% tall. The stem meets a separate low flattened circular bronze foot about 34% of canvas wide, finishing at 81% of the canvas height. Bold enough to survive small sizes, elegant and balanced. Lamp overall around 64% canvas height. The stem is straight, not flared, not sculptural, not organic.
Materials and light: The shade is warm ivory translucent linen/parchment, glowing from a hidden bulb inside. Beautiful warm cream center and honey-amber edges, with very subtle vertical fabric structure to make it an actual lampshade, no conspicuous pleats or noise. Its lower opening emits soft pale golden light, and you see a gentle dark rim around the opening. Stem and foot are satin aged brass with warm highlights and distinct darker shading. Real convincing dimensional shading. A soft restrained localized halo behind the shade and small warm light pool beneath the foot. Deep nearly black warm corners (#0E0C0A). Keep the silhouette crisp and dark negative space strong.
Style/medium: premium tactile 3D iOS app icon, a simplified beautifully crafted real bedside lamp. Just enough detail to identify the familiar object, not a room photograph. Very clean image. Warm reassuring mood.
Palette: near-black brown, warm amber (#F0A868), apricot (#FFC593), ivory and aged brass. No red, pink, purple, blue.
Composition: single lamp only, no furniture, no environment, no cable, no pull chain, no extra symbols. FULL SQUARE edge-to-edge opaque background, 1024x1024. No rounded exterior corners, no enclosing tile, no frames, no text, no watermark.
Avoid: MUSHROOM, toadstool, dome shade, bell, monolithic lamp, thick flared stem, illustration, flat vector, baby, fetus, moon, flower, heart, letters, stopwatch.
```

## Light

Keep a restrained ivory glow under the shade. The straight stem and separate circular foot remain satin bronze/brass with darker honey and brown midtones so they contrast cleanly against the pale background. Natural delicate contact shadow under the foot, no luminous outside halo. No texture noise or new details.
```text
Strictly preserve the shape, dimensions, placement and straight-sided lampshade, straight metal stem and separate foot from the source image. Do not reinterpret or redesign the object, do not make a dome or mushroom.
One finished full square opaque app icon, 1024x1024, no enclosing tile, no outer rounded corners, no border, no frame, no text, no watermarks, no room setting or other objects. Warm spectrum only.
```
