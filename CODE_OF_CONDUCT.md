# Code of conduct

Hushlight is built for people at one of the most vulnerable hours of their lives.
The project asks contributors to bring the same care to each other.

- Be kind and assume good faith. Critique the work, not the person.
- Be careful when talking about pregnancy, birth and loss. Not every labor ends well,
  and people reading an issue may know that firsthand.
- No harassment, insults, or sexual or discriminatory language, in any space this
  project uses.
- Medical claims need sources. Opinions are welcome; diagnosis is not.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules, and
may block people who keep breaking them. To raise a concern privately, use
**Security ▸ Report a vulnerability** and say it's about conduct, or email the
address on the [support page](https://christianwainright.com/hushlight/support).
